# HR AI Prompts

Plain-English prompts for HR AI work. Draft-only, role-based, and honest about what is not known.

Maintained by Boubacar Barry.

## The prompts

All ten are in PROMPTS.md.

1. **Score an HR task as an AI candidate**: Score an HR task out of 12 on AI fit and name the smallest safe first test.
2. **Review a planned AI use for risk**: Rate bias, privacy, oversight, consultation and record-keeping risk of a planned AI use.
3. **Redact text before using AI**: Replace names with roles and remove identifying detail before using AI.
4. **Verify an AI draft claim by claim**: Mark each claim in an AI draft verified, unverified or wrong against your source.
5. **Estimate value conservatively**: Low, middle and high value estimate with assumptions listed.
6. **Stress-test a plan**: Five-voice test plus a failure story, ending in a verdict.
7. **Run an expert panel on a decision**: Seven role seats and a contrarian review a decision, then give go, go-with-changes or stop.
8. **Draft a message to employees**: Message, FAQ and manager points for a change, with translation flags.
9. **Answer a policy question from policy text**: Answer an employee question from supplied policy text only, quoting the passage.
10. **Turn a long update into a leader brief**: Headline, ask, evidence and risk in under 150 words.

## How to use them

1. Open PROMPTS.md and pick a prompt.
2. Copy it into your AI tool.
3. Replace the [BRACKETED] part with your own text. Remove personal detail first.
4. Read the answer as a draft. Check facts and numbers yourself before anyone relies on it.

Nothing here is legal advice. Legal points the prompts ask for are marked: check current law and counsel.

If your tool can import skills, the companion repository hr-ai-skills has the longer, step-by-step versions. These prompts do not depend on it.
