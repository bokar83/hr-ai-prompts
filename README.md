# HR AI Prompts

Plain-English prompts for HR AI work. Draft-only, role-based, and honest about what is not known.

Maintained by Boubacar Barry.

## The prompts

One file per prompt, in the Prompts folder.

1. **[Score an HR task as an AI candidate](Prompts/01-score-an-hr-task-as-an-ai-candidate.md)**: Score an HR task out of 12 on AI fit and name the smallest safe first test.
2. **[Review a planned AI use for risk](Prompts/02-review-a-planned-ai-use-for-risk.md)**: Rate bias, privacy, oversight, consultation and record-keeping risk of a planned AI use.
3. **[Redact text before using AI](Prompts/03-redact-text-before-using-ai.md)**: Replace names with roles and remove identifying detail before using AI.
4. **[Verify an AI draft claim by claim](Prompts/04-verify-an-ai-draft-claim-by-claim.md)**: Mark each claim in an AI draft verified, unverified or wrong against your source.
5. **[Estimate value conservatively](Prompts/05-estimate-value-conservatively.md)**: Low, middle and high value estimate with assumptions listed.
6. **[Stress-test a plan](Prompts/06-stress-test-a-plan.md)**: Five-voice test plus a failure story, ending in a verdict.
7. **[Run an expert panel on a decision](Prompts/07-run-an-expert-panel-on-a-decision.md)**: Seven role seats and a contrarian review a decision, then give go, go-with-changes or stop.
8. **[Draft a message to employees](Prompts/08-draft-a-message-to-employees.md)**: Message, FAQ and manager points for a change, with translation flags.
9. **[Answer a policy question from policy text](Prompts/09-answer-a-policy-question-from-policy-text.md)**: Answer an employee question from supplied policy text only, quoting the passage.
10. **[Turn a long update into a leader brief](Prompts/10-turn-a-long-update-into-a-leader-brief.md)**: Headline, ask, evidence and risk in under 150 words.

## How to use them

1. Open the Prompts folder and pick a prompt file.
2. Copy it into your AI tool.
3. Replace the [BRACKETED] part with your own text. Remove personal detail first.
4. Read the answer as a draft. Check facts and numbers yourself before anyone relies on it.

Nothing here is legal advice. Legal points the prompts ask for are marked: check current law and counsel.

If your tool can import skills, the companion repository hr-ai-skills has the longer, step-by-step versions. These prompts do not depend on it.
